Privacy & Cookie Notice

Last updated: 31 October 2026

This notice explains the limited browser storage and optional website analytics used on the Aakruti Enterprises website. You can browse the website and contact Aakruti without accepting optional analytics.

Essential browser storage

The website uses your browser's local storage to remember whether you accepted or rejected optional analytics. This preference storage is necessary to respect your choice. The website does not currently set advertising cookies or use third-party advertising trackers.

Optional analytics — only with consent

Analytics is disabled by default. It starts only after you select Accept analytics in the consent notice. If you reject optional analytics or choose Essential only, analytics events are not sent.

When enabled, the website may send the following limited event information to an analytics endpoint operated for Aakruti: event type (such as page view, category view, product view or add-to-cart), an anonymous browser identifier, page path, broad device category, campaign/referral source hostname, and the category or product involved. The analytics identifier is stored in your browser only after you accept.

Information analytics is not intended to collect

The analytics feature is not designed to collect your name, phone number, email address, enquiry form contents, precise location, age, gender or income. Please do not put personal information into website URLs or campaign parameters.

Where analytics data is processed

When you consent, event data is sent to the website's first-party analytics route and may be forwarded to Google Apps Script for storage in a Google Sheet managed by the website owner. Google may process technical information as part of providing its services. Access to the spreadsheet should be limited to authorized business personnel. Analytics records may remain in the owner's spreadsheet after you withdraw consent; withdrawal stops future analytics events from your browser but does not automatically delete historical records.

Change or withdraw your choice

You can change your selection at any time using the Cookie settings link in the website footer. Choosing Reject optional or Essential only stops future optional analytics events. You can also clear this website's local storage in your browser settings to reset the saved preference; the consent notice will then appear again.
