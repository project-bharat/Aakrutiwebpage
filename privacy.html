Privacy Notice

Website analytics and cookie choices

Aakruti Enterprises uses essential browser storage to remember your optional analytics choice. If you accept analytics, this website sends limited usage events to a Google Sheet managed by the website owner to understand which pages and furniture categories are useful.

What optional analytics records

Analytics may record the event time, an anonymous browser identifier, page path and title, referring website hostname, broad device category, screen dimensions, browser language, campaign source label, furniture category viewed, and product interaction events.

What this feature does not intentionally collect

This analytics feature is not designed to collect your name, phone number, email address, exact location, IP address, age, gender, income, or the contents of enquiry forms. Please do not include personal information in URLs or campaign parameters.

Your choice

You can reject optional analytics and continue to use the website. If you accepted and want to withdraw consent, clear this website's local storage/site data in your browser; future analytics events will stop unless you accept again. Previously recorded rows may remain in the owner's analytics spreadsheet and are subject to the owner's retention practices.

Storage and third-party services

When enabled, anonymous event data is sent to Google Apps Script and stored in Google Sheets under the website owner's Google account. Google may process technical information as part of providing its services. Access to the spreadsheet should be restricted to authorized team members.

Contact

For questions about this notice or website data, contact Aakruti Enterprises using the contact details shown on the main website.

This is a starter notice and should be reviewed and adapted to your actual business contact details, retention period, and applicable legal requirements before relying on it as a complete legal policy.
