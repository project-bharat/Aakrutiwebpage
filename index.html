Our story

A journey shaped by experience. A vision shaped around you.

Every meaningful space begins with a vision. Since 1992, Aakruti Enterprises has been part of that journey, building its understanding of furniture, functionality and the details that make a space feel complete.

What began as a foundation of experience has evolved into a dedicated furniture manufacturing journey, beginning in 2008. Today, from our manufacturing facility in Hinjewadi, Pune, we bring together skilled people, modern machinery and a thoughtful approach to making furniture.

But for us, creating furniture is not simply about dimensions, materials or finishes. It begins with listening — understanding how you live, what your space needs and what matters to you.

From the first conversation to the final finish, we believe every detail deserves consideration. Through personalised consultation, practical expertise and an appreciation for individual preferences, we work to create furniture that belongs naturally in your space.

A legacy built over time. A personal approach to every space.

FAQs and policies

A few useful things to know.

Order-specific terms are shared and confirmed in your quotation before production begins.

Where is Aakruti Enterprises located?

We are a furniture manufacturer in Aakruti Enterprises, Hulavale Vasti, Hinjewadi, Taluka Mulshi, District Pune, Maharashtra 411057. Appointments are preferred; call or WhatsApp +91 83087 64140.

What furniture do you make?

We make beds, wardrobes, coffee and centre tables, side tables, TV units, study and office furniture, outdoor furniture and custom furniture, all made to order.

Do you make furniture to my size and design?

Yes. You can discuss dimensions, colours and finishes, or share a reference image. We confirm feasibility and the final specification with your quotation.

How long does it take?

Typical lead time is approximately 3–4 weeks. The final timeline depends on the product, customisation and workload, and is confirmed with the quotation.

Do you charge for consultation or delivery?

Consultation and delivery are generally free within Pune, PCMC and nearby areas. For other locations, travel and transportation charges apply and are confirmed in advance.

How does payment work?

Payment can be made by cash, UPI, bank transfer or cheque. The usual schedule is an advance before production starts, a further instalment when material reaches the site or during installation, and the balance after completion. Exact amounts and dates are confirmed in your quotation.

Do you offer a warranty?

Warranty support and after-sales service are available. Terms and coverage are confirmed with the order.

Can I cancel an order?

Orders cannot be cancelled once work has started. Please confirm your specifications before approving production.

What are your working hours?

Monday to Saturday, 9:00 AM to 5:30 PM. Closed on Sunday.

Do you work with architects and design professionals?

Yes. We manufacture furniture for architects, designers, builders, offices and hospitality projects, subject to feasibility.
