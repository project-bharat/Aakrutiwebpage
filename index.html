Who We Are

Setting the Benchmark in Luxury Furniture Manufacturing in Pune

Based in the heart of Hinjewadi, Pune, Aakruti Enterprises is a premier full-scale interior furniture manufacturer and turnkey supplier. Under the visionary leadership of Proprietor Rushi Chavhan, we bridge the gap between bespoke Italian-inspired aesthetics and heavy-duty Indian craftsmanship.

Whether you are a homeowner furnishing a luxury villa or penthouse, an interior designer/architect sourcing custom pieces, or a corporate organization setting up an executive workspace in Hinjewadi IT Park, our factory delivers tailor-made solutions at direct-manufacturer pricing.

Grade-A Raw Materials

Seasoned Teakwood, BWP Marine Ply, HDHMR, Italian Marble tops, and high-GSM stain-resistant fabrics.

100% Custom Dimensions

Every catalogue item can be customized in size, polish shade, fabric texture, and hardware finish.
